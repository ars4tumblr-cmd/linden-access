Linden

A private assistant for the owner's documents, email and calendar.

Linden is a private, owner-operated assistant. Its Google connection accesses only accounts explicitly authorized by their owner. This website describes that connection; it does not provide public registration or access to the assistant.

What the connection does

With consent, Linden can read and organize Gmail, work with Google Drive files, Google Docs and Google Sheets, and manage Google Calendar. The owner must approve each specific outgoing email, deletion, file-sharing change and calendar mutation before the assistant performs it.

Those action-confirmation rules govern assistant behavior; Google's granted OAuth scopes remain broader technical capabilities. The owner can withdraw Google access at any time in Google Account connections.

Data handling

Task-relevant content may be processed by configured AI providers to perform the owner's requests. The privacy policy describes those transfers, storage and controls. Linden does not collect Google passwords.

Linden is not a Google product and is not endorsed by Google.
