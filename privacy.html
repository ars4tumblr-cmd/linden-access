Linden · Personal use

Privacy policy

Effective: 31 October 2026. This policy describes the owner's private Linden deployment. External services have their own policies.

Data and purposes

After explicit Google OAuth consent, Linden may access Gmail messages and related mailbox information, files and metadata in Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets, and calendar information in Google Calendar. It uses this information to perform assistance requested by the owner. The connected services and actual OAuth scopes are shown on Google's consent screen.

Outgoing email, deletion, file sharing and calendar mutations require the owner's approval of the specific action. OAuth credentials grant technical access; the approval rule is an assistant operating policy.

Model processing and other services

Task-relevant content may be transmitted to the configured model provider, currently DeepSeek or Groq, to produce responses and operate tools. The Telegram interface and owner-selected tools may also process content needed for the requested task. These services have their own terms, security and retention policies. Broad Google consent is not permission to copy an entire mailbox or Drive to a model provider without a user request.

Google API Limited Use

Linden's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The operator is responsible for using downstream services and settings consistently with these restrictions.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, task files and conversation state are stored on the owner's controlled server. Protected recovery copies may be stored on the owner's computer. Current generated backup archives are rotated by age, count and size, with up to seven days on the server and fourteen days on the owner's computer. These backup limits do not automatically delete live conversation history or task files.

Credentials are retained while the connection is in use or until removed by the owner. Local data and live history remain until the owner deletes them. Data held by external providers is subject to their independent policies.

Security and control

Linden does not collect the owner's Google password. It uses revocable OAuth tokens, restricted file permissions, a separate agent environment and protected backups. No system can guarantee absolute security.

The owner can revoke access at Google Account connections and remove local credentials and stored content. Existing recovery archives must also be removed or allowed to expire under their retention rules if their copies are no longer wanted.

Contact and changes

Contact: Contact form. Update this policy when the deployment's data uses, providers or retention practices change.

The contact form is hosted by Google Forms. It collects your message and, if you choose to provide it, a reply email. Submissions are available to the owner and authorized administrators, not published as a public discussion. Do not include credentials or sensitive documents. Messages are retained until the owner removes them; Google's own processing is governed by its policies.
